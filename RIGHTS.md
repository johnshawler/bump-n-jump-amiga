# Rights, provenance and third-party dependencies

This repository is publicly viewable, but no blanket license is assigned to the recovered port code or documentation. A licensing grant from the relevant authors has not been established. Publication does not assert ownership of third-party material or authorize reuse beyond rights otherwise available to you.

Original arcade and NES game names, code, graphics, music and trademarks remain with their respective rights holders. Original cartridge files, generated translations of cartridge code, extracted data and playable builds are deliberately excluded. Credits are attribution, not a license.

The Python tools depend on py65, Pillow, NumPy and amitools. These dependencies are not vendored. `py65-LICENSE.txt` preserves the BSD notice included in the recovered source packages; it applies to py65, not the whole project. Toolchain, Amiga SDK, FS-UAE and WHDLoad rights remain with their respective authors. No third-party executable, emulator firmware or Workbench files are included.

Further licensing decisions require establishing the provenance and permissions for the particular material; do not infer an MIT/GPL/BSD license for the port from a dependency's license.
